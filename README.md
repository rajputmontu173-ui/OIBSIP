# Oasis Infobyte - Data Analytics Task 1

## Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis

### Project Overview

This project is completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this project is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on retail sales data and identify useful business insights from the dataset.

### Objectives

* Understand the retail sales dataset.
* Check dataset shape, columns and data types.
* Check missing values.
* Calculate descriptive statistics.
* Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
* Analyze customer demographics.
* Identify top-selling products.
* Analyze revenue by category.
* Study correlations between numerical variables.
* Compare sales through different channels.
* Provide business insights and actionable recommendations.

### Dataset

The dataset contains **1,500 retail transactions** and **12 columns**.

Main columns include:

* Transaction ID
* Customer ID
* Product
* Category
* Price Per Unit
* Quantity
* Total Sales
* Date
* Age
* Gender
* Channel
* Payment Method

### Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn



### Analysis Performed

#### 1. Dataset Overview

Checked the number of rows and columns, column names, data types and missing values.

#### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Calculated count, mean, standard deviation, minimum, maximum and quartile values for numerical columns.

#### 3. Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales trends for each month of 2025.

#### 4. Quarterly Sales Analysis

Compared total sales across Q1, Q2, Q3 and Q4.

#### 5. Customer Demographics

Analyzed customer age and gender distribution.

#### 6. Product Analysis

Identified the top 10 products based on total sales.

#### 7. Category Analysis

Compared revenue generated by different product categories.

#### 8. Correlation Analysis

Created a correlation heatmap for price, quantity, total sales and customer age.

#### 9. Sales Channel Analysis

Compared sales generated through Online and Store channels.

### Key Results

* Total Sales: **62,625,038**
* Total Transactions: **1,500**
* Total Quantity Sold: **4,460**
* Unique Customers: **248**
* Highest Selling Product: **Laptop**
* Highest Revenue Category: **Electronics**
* Highest Revenue Month: **December 2025**
* Best Sales Channel: **Online**

### Business Recommendations

1. Increase inventory for high-performing product categories.
2. Run special offers and marketing campaigns during high-sales months.
3. Focus promotions on the stronger sales channel and high-performing products.

### Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of retail sales performance and customer behavior. The findings can help businesses understand their high-performing products, categories, sales periods and sales channels, which can support better inventory and marketing decisions.
